Based on Raion Light's Murasama Standalone.

The original needs four separate keys on mobile. This version folds them into two core buttons (plus an optional dedicated ground-smash key) and reworks most of the hitboxes and numbers:

- **Art key**: hold to charge, tap when full to throw the blade, tap again while it is out to dash in.
- **Finisher key**: tap in the air for the aerial slash (reflects enemy projectiles); tap on the ground for the ground smash.
- **Smash slot is a choice**: in ModConfig you can swap Ground Smash for **Getsuga Tensho** — a finisher slash followed by a huge crescent projectile.
- **23 skins**, all sharing one recipe (Copper Shortsword + 48 iron/lead bars + 16 fallen stars at an anvil), each with its own palette, UI bars and item name colour.
- **Progression**: panel damage starts at 15 and grows to 70 with boss kills, crit 4% → 34%; skills unlock as you progress.
- **Localization**: full Chinese, including tooltips, config entries and changelog.
