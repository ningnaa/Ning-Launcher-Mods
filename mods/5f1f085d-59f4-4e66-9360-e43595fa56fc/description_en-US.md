Based on Raion Light's Murasama Standalone.

## Buttons
- **Combat Art** — hold to charge when empty, tap when full to throw the blade; tap again while it is out to dash.
- **Finisher** — tap in the air for Kuuenzan; tap on the ground for Ground Smash.
- **Ground Smash** — its own button.
- **Getsuga** — its own button: a third sweeping slash, then a crescent wave.
- **Cyclone** — hold in the air to spin; ends on landing.

## Combat
- **Sweeping Edge** — double damage.
- **Getsuga** — 500% panel damage; the shader box grows with attack range.
- **Dimensional Slash** — 1024% panel damage with a full-screen rift.
- **Ground Smash / Kuuenzan / Cyclone / Dash** — 75% / 80% / 60% / 125%.
- Every hitbox and slash scales with the Attack Range stat.

## Skins
23 Murasama skins with one recipe (Copper Shortsword + 48 Iron/Lead Bars + 16 Fallen Stars at an Iron Anvil). Each has its own palette, UI bars and name colour; item tooltips (damage / crit / speed / knockback) follow the blade colour, and Rainbow is a flowing per-letter rainbow.

## Misc
- Prefix size bonus multiplies reach and hitbox.
