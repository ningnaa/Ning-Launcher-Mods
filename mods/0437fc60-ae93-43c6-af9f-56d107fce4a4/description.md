# Starfall Relic

One pack that holds everything we've built: **five weapons, one boss, one growing accessory**.

## What's inside

**Weapons**
- **Divine Angel Sword** (melee, leveling) — four-hit combo; +1 level per 30 kills (bosses +3~8), no level cap. Crafted **early**: 12 Silver/Tungsten Bars + 10 Fallen Stars + 5 Silk @ Anvil.
- **Black Hole Staff** (magic) — six arcing bolts that converge, then a micro black hole; skill key drops a devouring black hole. After **Skeletron Prime**.
- **Phase Bow** (ranged) — charge-and-release piercing arrows; every 24 shots triggers a multi-shot drone volley; skill key calls an orbital beam sweep. After **The Twins**.
- **Star Rail Whelp** (summon) — summons a small star dragon per minion slot; skill key calls the adult dragon. After **The Destroyer**.
- **Void Fish Cannon** (ranged) — hold to fire; six barrels cycle, each volley eats 6 bullets; skill key tears a rift that pulls enemies in and unloads on them. After **all three mechanical bosses**.

**Boss** — **Shadow Wanderer**: teleports, fires lasers, summons servants. Its material crafts its own summon item so you can re-fight it.

**Accessory** — **Tear Star**: a pendant that grows with you. Four tiers; every kill teaches it 3 random stats out of 25, **all uncapped**. Wear it and it works; there is a HUD panel and a per-stat toggle in the config. Dying does not drop it — progress resets to zero instead.

## Notes

- Requires **Bunny Loader** 0.4.x (Terraria 1.4.5.8.6).
- Do **not** enable the standalone versions of these mods at the same time — they would register twice.
