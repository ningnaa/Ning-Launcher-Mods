# Tempest of Many Forms

Defeat enemies during early progression for a chance to find a Fractured Star Core. The core drops only once per world. Craft the bow by hand with the core, then tap the star eye above the screen to switch between Gale, Blazing Ember, Starwheel, and Finality. Defeating bosses unlocks ten stages.
