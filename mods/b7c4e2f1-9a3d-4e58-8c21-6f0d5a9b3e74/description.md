# Summoner Expanded

A summoner mod for Terraria mobile.

## Star's Blessing

A summoner accessory that grows with your boss progression. Available from the
first night, and it keeps getting stronger.

**How to get it:** a Work Bench and one Fallen Star. Fallen Stars drop from the
sky at night — you can pick one up on your very first evening.

**What it gives at the start:** +1 minion slot, +1 sentry slot, +10% summon
damage, +30% movement speed.

**How it grows:** every boss stage you clear makes it permanently stronger. When
a stage lists several bosses, clearing any one of them counts.

| Stage | Bosses | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | King Slime / Eye of Cthulhu | +8% damage |
| 2 | Eater of Worlds / Brain of Cthulhu | +1 slot, +8% damage |
| 3 | Queen Bee / Skeletron / Deerclops | +1 sentry, +10% damage |
| 4 | Wall of Flesh | +10% damage, +20 life |
| 5 | Queen Slime / Mechs / Duke Fishron | +1 slot, +1 sentry, +12% damage |
| 6 | Plantera | +9% damage, +20 life |
| 7 | Golem / Empress of Light | +1 slot, +1 sentry, +11% damage, +4 defense |
| 8 | Lunatic Cultist | +10% damage, +4 defense |
| 9 | Moon Lord | +1 slot, +1 sentry, +12% damage, +40 life, +8 defense |

**Full clear:** +5 minion slots, +5 sentry slots, +100% summon damage, +80 max
life, +16 defense, +100% movement speed.

**The tooltip tells you where to go next.** Each uncleared stage shows a riddle
that describes the boss's domain and its tells without naming the summoning
item. Clear everything and the last line tells you the star sea has gone quiet.

## Vine Charm

Vanilla gives a summoner **no accessory at all** before the Wall of Flesh — the
first one is the Summoner Emblem, which is a hardmode drop. This is the piece
that fills that gap.

**How to get it:** a Tinkerer's Workshop, 10 Vines and 6 Jungle Spores.

Vines drop from Man Eaters and Angry Trappers in the jungle; Jungle Spores grow
on the grass in the underground jungle. No boss required for either.

**What it gives:** +1 minion slot, −12% summon damage.

The extra minion is real damage; the cost is that each one hits slightly softer.
It is strongest when you have few slots and grows milder as you stack more.

## Corrupt Pendant / Crimson Pendant

Summoner accessories for the window right after the evil boss. One per world.

**How to get it (Corruption world):** a Tinkerer's Workshop, 8 Demonite Bar and 5 Shadow Scale.
**How to get it (Crimson world):** a Tinkerer's Workshop, 8 Crimtane Bar and 5 Tissue Sample.

The bars are smelted from the ore, and Shadow Scale and Tissue Sample come from the Eater of Worlds and the Brain of Cthulhu — so the evil boss has to be down first.

**Corrupt Pendant:** +20% summon damage.
**Crimson Pendant:** +15% summon damage, +20% max life.

Corruption buys damage, Crimson buys margin. Each is only craftable in its own
world, so the other one needs a world swap.

## Hive Charm

A summoner accessory for after Queen Bee. It fills the last real gap in the
class: **vanilla summoners have no way to heal at all**.

**How to get it:** a Tinkerer's Workshop, 10 Hive, 5 Bottled Honey and 15 Bee Wax.

Bee Wax only drops from Queen Bee, 16–26 at a time — so 15 Bee Wax means
**Queen Bee has to be down first**. Hive and Bottled Honey are both found in the
Bee Hive biome.

**What it gives:** +1 minion slot, +1 sentry slot, and a 10% chance for minion
hits to restore 3 life.

That is not a small amount — over a whole fight it comes to four or five healing
potions. But it only fires on hits: miss, and you get nothing. So it rewards
standing inside your minions' reach rather than standing still.

## Slime Staff recipe

In vanilla the Slime Staff is a 0.01% drop, which leaves an early summoner with
no weapon. This adds a reliable path:

**A Work Bench and 15 Gel.**

The vanilla drop rate and every existing recipe are untouched.

## Pink Slime Staff

The pink version of the Slime Staff. Same summoner weapon, same baby slime, more
damage — and the minion flies alongside you instead of hopping.

**How to get it:**
- Corruption world: a Work Bench, 10 Pink Gel, 5 Shadow Scale, 8 Demonite Bar
- Crimson world: a Work Bench, 10 Pink Gel, 5 Tissue Sample, 8 Crimtane Bar

Pink Gel drops from Pinky in stacks of 25–50. Shadow Scale and Tissue Sample
come from the Eater of Worlds and the Brain of Cthulhu, and the bars are smelted
from their ore — so the staff needs the evil boss down first.

| | Slime Staff | Pink Slime Staff |
|---|---|---|
| Damage | 8 | **13** |

No mana cost. The Baby Pink Slime floats next to you, rams enemies, and flies
over when it falls behind.

## Flinx Hood

Vanilla ships only the Flinx Fur Coat, so the set is always half-finished. This
adds the head:

**A Loom, 6 Flinx Fur and 4 Silk.** +1 defense, +5% summon damage.

## Flinx Boots

The leg slot, to complete the set:

**A Loom, 8 Flinx Fur and 5 Silk.** +1 defense, +5% summon damage, +5%
movement speed.

All three pieces worn together: **+10% summon damage**.
